Android · Headwind enrollment

Setting up the tag on a phone

Three phases: back everything up, reset the phone and enroll it, then restore what belongs on it. Do them in order — the reset in phase two erases the phone completely, so nothing after that point can save what you skipped backing up first.

Written so a parent can follow it phone-in-hand. The + sections are technical detail for whoever's running the enrollment — safe to skip if that's not you.

1 · Back everything up

Ten minutes now saves every photo, chat, and app record. Do all of this before you touch Settings → Reset.

⚠ The reset in phase 2 is a real factory reset

It erases the phone — photos, messages, app logins, everything — back to how it left the store. Android has no undo for that. This phase is the only chance to save what's on it.

+ How do I check what's actually on the phone — and what will actually be backed up — before I reset it?

These are two different questions, and stock Android's UI doesn't answer either one cleanly. Settings → Google → Backup shows a last-backup date and a rough size, not an itemized list.

To see every app currently installed: enable Developer Options (Settings → About phone → tap Build number seven times), turn on USB debugging, plug the phone into a computer with adb installed, and run:

every user-installed app 
adb shell pm list packages -3

To see which of those apps will actually be included in the backup — not every app qualifies; an app opts in or out of Android's Auto Backup in its own manifest, and there's no setting screen that lists this — run:

backup eligibility per app, plus current transport status 
adb shell dumpsys backup

The output lists each package with its backup participation. Apps that opt out — most banking apps, some games — show up here even though they were never going to make it into the Google backup regardless of how you back up. That's the honest list of what phase 1's Google-backup step will and won't carry; the WhatsApp and photo/video steps exist precisely to cover the two biggest things that never show up in that output.

2 · Reset the phone and enroll it

Only start this once every box above is checked.

ⓘ Enrollment only works during setup

Device Owner provisioning has to happen before the phone reaches its home screen — that's the only window Android allows it. If the phone already reached the home screen, it needs another factory reset for a second try.

3 · Restore what belongs on it

Some of this happens by itself. Some needs a manual step — and once the tag is enrolled, "automatic" means something narrower than it used to.

⚠ Does it reinstall apps even with Headwind on? Not quite the way a normal phone does.

On a normal phone, signing into your Google account after a reset silently reinstalls everything you had before. Once Headwind is enrolled as Device Owner, that stops being the whole story, in two ways:

1. QR/managed provisioning skips the usual restore screen. The setup flow used to make a phone a Device Owner is a deliberately stripped-down path — it does not run the normal "copy apps & data from backup" step consumer setup shows. Signing in afterward still syncs contacts and settings, but the blanket "reinstall my whole app list" behavior isn't part of that path the way it is on an unmanaged phone.

2. Even where an app does get pushed, it's Headwind deciding, not Google. Only apps allowed in this phone's current rung (see app_rules in the tag's policy) get installed — regardless of what was on the phone before the reset, and regardless of what's sitting in the Google backup. An app that isn't on the allowlist for this rung will not quietly come back just because it used to be installed. That's not a gap — it's the tag doing exactly what it's for.

Exact behavior can vary a little by Android OEM, which is exactly why SETUP-PHONES.md has you verify this on one throwaway phone before enrolling anybody for real.

Quick answers

Can I check a phone's apps before resetting it?

Yes — see the "how do I check what's on the phone" box in phase 1. Short version: adb shell pm list packages -3 lists everything installed; adb shell dumpsys backup shows which of those apps actually participate in Android's backup — which is usually the more useful question, since not every installed app opts in.

Will it reinstall apps even with Headwind on?

Only the ones the tag's current policy allows for that phone — see the callout in phase 3. Enrollment changes what "automatic restore" means: it's no longer "whatever was there before," it's "whatever this rung allows," pushed by Headwind rather than by Google's own restore flow.
