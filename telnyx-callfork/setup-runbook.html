Call-Fork Setup Runbook

One US number rings both Israeli phones. Nobody answers the flip — you reject and dial back on free plan minutes. Work top to bottom; each phase unlocks the next. Tick boxes as you go — they're saved on this device.

Why the design looks like this

US DID rental~$1 / mo
Inbound to the US DID$0.005 / min
Answer on the iPhone softphone (SIP)$0 · free
Flip rings but you reject it$0 · free
Flip answers (or voicemail picks up)$0.1096 / min · 60/60

Billing starts on answer, never on ring. The whole system exists to keep that last red line from ever happening. Target spend: about a dollar a month plus pennies.

◉ The green key on the flip is the $0.11 key Pressing Call to answer on the Nokia is the only action in the system that costs real money — it triggers the 60-second minimum at $0.1096/min. Build one habit: red to reject, then dial back. Every other outcome — reject, ignore, phone off, no coverage — is free.
1Deploy the worker
dash.cloudflare.com · browser only▶

No download, no terminal. Cloudflare pulls the code straight from your GitHub repo (already merged to main).

If the build shows a red error, screenshot it — subfolder builds occasionally need one tweak.

2Add the 5 secrets
worker → settings → variables▶

In the new Worker → Settings → Variables and Secrets → Add. Choose Encrypt for each so it's a secret, not plain text. Never paste these into the repo.

SecretValue
TELNYX_US_DIDyour US DID, +1…
SIP_URIsip:usersupport11004@sip.telnyx.com

IPHONE_HOT_NUMBERthe iPhone's HOT number, +972…
TELNYX_PUBLIC_KEYTelnyx → Account Settings → Keys & Credentials → Public Key
3Health check
your worker URL▶

Open https://<your-worker>.workers.dev/health in the browser.

Paste this output back to me and I'll confirm it's green before you spend a cent on the DID.

4Wire Telnyx to the worker
portal.telnyx.com▶

You've already built the SIP Connection (flipforwarding), the TeXML app (callforwarding), and added Israel to the Outbound Voice Profile. This phase connects them and points the DID at the right object.

▲ The one wiring mistake that breaks everything silently If the DID is assigned to the SIP Connection instead of the TeXML app, inbound calls skip the worker entirely and there's no error to tell you why. Numbers → My Numbers → your DID → Connection/Application = callforwarding.
5Set up the phones
iPhone · Nokia · both HOT lines▶

Turn push / background registration ON — if the app sleeps, the SIP leg won't ring and you'll take the expensive path more often. Confirm it shows registered under the SIP Connection.

▲ Voicemail is a billed answer If HOT voicemail picks up a forked call, that's an answered leg at $0.1096 with the 60-second minimum — the exact charge this system avoids, happening silently. Verify: call each Israeli number and let it ring out. You want the "not answering" tone, not a greeting.

Save the DID in full international form (+1…). If your HOT bundle rate is tied to the 017 prefix, save it as 0171… instead so the callback comes out of the bundle.

6Test — before touching T-Mobile
any phone → the DID▶

Call the Telnyx DID directly from any phone and walk the whole path. No real minutes at risk — you control every leg.

If a leg misbehaves, Telnyx Voice → Debugging shows per-leg SIP results. Send me what you see and I'll read it.

7Turn on T-Mobile forwarding
the US phone · do this last▶

Only once Phase 6 works end to end. Doing this last means a broken forward can't strand your real US number.

Dial on the US phoneDoes
**21*<DID>#Enable unconditional forwarding to the DID
*#21#Check current forwarding status
##21#Disable forwarding
##002#Clear all forwarding rules

Use unconditional (**21*), not busy/no-answer forwarding, so the T-Mobile SIM never rings and never involves its own voicemail.
